Defect: **s.182 is complete on giving the information with the stated intent or knowledge; no actual consequence is required.** The text is "intending thereby to cause, or knowing it to be likely that he will thereby cause, such public servant (a) to do or omit … or (b) to use the lawful power …". The file's limb elements (`misdirectedOfficialAct`: "the public servant does or omits …"; `abuseOfLawfulPower`: "the public servant's lawful power is used …") require the consequence to have happened, and the `limbAMissingOutcome` / `limbBMissingOutcome` guards assert that a knowing false report with the right intent but no resulting act is an omission. Under the statute that reading is the offence. False refusal on every completed-on-utterance case.

Required fix:
1. Reword the limb elements as the object of the mental element, not an event: (a) `intendedConsequenceMisdirectedAct`: "the consequence intended, or known to be likely, is that the public servant do or omit something they ought not to do or omit if the true facts were known"; (b) `intendedConsequenceAbuseOfPower`: "the consequence intended, or known to be likely, is the use of the public servant's lawful power to the injury or annoyance of any person". `mentalElementAsToConsequence` then reads "intends, or knows it likely, that the false information will cause the consequence named by the limb" (no "outcome below").
2. Replace the two "missing outcome" guards with "intended or likely consequence not one the section names" (knowing false report, intent present, but the consequence contemplated is neither (a) nor (b)), and add a note in each `notFormalisable` that an actually occurring consequence is evidence of the mental element, never a required element.
3. Doc comment on `limbAMissingMentalElement` ("an honest mistake … given without any belief it was false") describes a missing `givesFalseInformation`, not a missing consequence element; reword to "false information given knowingly, with neither intent nor awareness of any likely official consequence".

## BNS 69 ruling on encoding — 2026-09-15, via lead

Track A objects that `notRape` in `required` plus `amountsToRape` in `denials` encodes one condition twice (silent → omitted, asserted → refuted; double-fires under Phase 2b), the pattern it removed from IPC 416. Accepted: one condition, one encoding. My earlier "keep both" is withdrawn.

Legal ruling: **denial form (a).** "such sexual intercourse not amounting to the offence of rape" is a scope carve-out: BNS 69 is the residual offence for deceit-induced intercourse that is not rape, and the clause excludes conduct already caught by BNS 63/64. It is not an ingredient the reading must establish; a reading silent on rape does not omit anything. So: `amountsToRape` in `denials` with the clause quoted beside it; `notRape` removed from `required` and from `axioms`; the `missingNotRape` guard removed; the refutation guard (inducement + intercourse + amountsToRape → `!check`, `Refuted`) stays; `notFormalisable` keeps the BNS 63 pointer and adds that the rape/not-rape boundary is adjudicated under BNS 63, not derived here. Contrast IPC 405: "dishonestly" is an element there and the good-faith illustration is a defeater of it; here the text states the exclusion directly, so protocol rule 2 lands on denial form.
